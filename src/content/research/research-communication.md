## Problem

The artifact matters: writing, visualizations, dashboards, and templates can change
how teams think together.

## Role

## Approach

Explain the workflow: how you turn analysis into usable communication, what tradeoffs
you make, and how you know the artifact is working.

## What Changed

better shared understanding.
